**Conversion Copy Deck**

Concierge Home-Dentist Landing Page: hero to footer

*How to use this deck: copy is written section by section in the order of the page. \[BRACKETS\] are details to fill in. \[CONFIRM\] marks a claim or service the dentist must verify is true before publishing. Nothing here invents ratings, awards or statistics.*

## **Voice and rules**

* Voice: warm, confident, calm. A trusted private physician, not a clinic ad and not a luxury brand shouting.

* Lead with relief (no traffic, no waiting room, no nerves), then proof, then one clear action.

* Write “gentle”, “unhurried”, “private”, “precise”. Avoid “surgical” and “painless”, which alarm anxious patients or promise too much.

* Avoid unprovable superlatives: “best”, “guaranteed”, “100%”, “zero risk”.

* One action per section: message on WhatsApp.

* Keep legal and regional wording neutral until the practice’s market and regulator are confirmed.
