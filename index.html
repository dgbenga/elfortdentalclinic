Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers to the questions clients ask before submitting a project brief.

How long does a project take?
Standard websites typically take 2 to 3 weeks. E-commerce platforms and web applications take 4 to 8 weeks depending on complexity. We agree on a guaranteed milestone timeline before starting.
How much does it cost?
We provide transparent, fixed starting rates: Standard Websites start from ₦100,000, E-commerce Platforms from ₦200,000, Web Applications from ₦300,000, and Custom Software from ₦500,000. Every build is quoted transparently upfront based on your brief with zero hidden fees.
What do I need to provide before we start?
Simply complete the project brief on our registration page, and provide a Google Drive folder link containing your logo, brand guidelines, and any copy or images you would like included.
Do you handle hosting and custom domains?
Yes. We set up your domain records, SSL security certificates, and cloud hosting infrastructure, and hand over complete admin ownership to your team upon launch.
What happens after launch?
Every build includes a 30-day post-launch warranty to fix any unexpected bugs. We also provide ongoing maintenance retainers and feature upgrade plans when your user base grows.
