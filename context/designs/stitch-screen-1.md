# Google Stitch Design Prompt: Screen 1 (Hero & Trust Architecture)

## 1. Project Visual Overview
* **Product Mission:** High-end concierge in-home dentistry delivering clinical-grade care in complete residential privacy.
* **Core Personas:** Discerning executives, busy parents, dental-phobic individuals, mobility-limited seniors.
* **Locked Palette:**
  - Canvas / Background: `#FAFAF9` (Warm Stone White)
  - Card Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` with border `#E7E5E4`
  - Text Primary: `#1C1917` (Deep Obsidian)
  - Text Secondary: `#57534E` (Muted Truffle)
  - Brand Accent: `#C5A059` (Champagne Gold)
  - Primary CTA: `#128C4A` (Forest Green with white text) or `#25D366` (with `#0B3D1E` text)
* **Atmospheric Mood:** Private physician meets 5-star boutique hospitality (Aman / Four Seasons). High editorial grace, generous breathing room, zero cold clinical dread.

---
